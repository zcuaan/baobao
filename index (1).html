ABOUT SPROUT GUIDANCE

Support for every member of the team.

Sprout is being designed for nursery practitioners, SENCOs, room leaders and managers. The aim is to make official guidance easier to find and understand without replacing the original documents.

What works today

  • Browse eight clear topics.
  • Find and open official government publication links.
  • Search the source directory by title and related keywords.
  • Adjust text size and line spacing.

What still needs connecting

AI answers, document importing and approval, citation checks, and user accounts all need a secure backend.

When enabled, AI should only answer from administrator-approved, indexed documents. Each substantive statement must cite the exact supporting passage. If evidence is missing, it should say so rather than guess.
